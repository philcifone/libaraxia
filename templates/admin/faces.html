Placeholders

Photos of the plain parts of things: a clamshell's plastic top and sides, an orange tape's bottom, a clear DVD case's edge, a book's pages. Upload them under their medium, then group them into named sets ("White clamshell", "Nickelodeon"). An owner picks a set when adding an item, or a set or single placeholders on its edit page; anything left unpicked keeps the plain look. Tops and bottoms are turned to lie wide and sides to stand tall as they upload.
