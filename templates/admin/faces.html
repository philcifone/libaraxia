Placeholders

Photos of the plain parts of things: a clamshell's plastic top and sides, an orange tape's bottom, a clear or black DVD case's edge, a book's pages. An item without a photo of a face shows the placeholder its owner picked, or else its kind's default set, or else the plain colour. Sets fill an item's faces in one go (Clamshell: top, right side and bottom). A VHS with a top photo is a sleeve; without one it's a clamshell, and uses that default.
