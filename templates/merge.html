Two records for one work, where one holds the history: a book from before owners existed with your reviews on it, beside the copy on your shelf; or an Audiobookshelf listen that came in as its own audiobook beside the paper copy.

Merging moves every review, status, reading session, tag, collection entry, wishlist entry, photo, loan and import link from one record onto the other, then deletes the empty one. Where both hold something only one can keep, you choose. Duplicates you own on purpose are never merged unless you ask.

Start from the record you want to keep: its edit page, Book tools → Merge.
