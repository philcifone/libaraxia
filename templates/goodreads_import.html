How it works

  1. Upload your export. Nothing is saved yet.
  2. Review it. Every row is sorted into a few boxes, each saying what will happen. Change anything you like, a row at a time or a whole selection.
  3. Import. Libaraxia backs up your library first. You can undo the whole import for 30 days (on the result, or in Profile, Recent bulk edits).

Back up first

An import can write hundreds of items at once, so Libaraxia backs up your library when you press Import (you can make one yourself first, too). Restoring a backup puts your library back exactly as it was and changes nobody else's data.

What happens to your Goodreads data

Books you read, not in Libaraxia
Come in as read, unshelved: in your reading stats and history, findable under Books, Show: Unshelved, never on your shelf. Choose "Add to my library" for the ones you do own. Read Count becomes that many reading sessions; Goodreads only keeps the date of the last one.
Books already in Libaraxia
Libaraxia wins. The import only adds what is missing: a status, rating, review or reading session. A Goodreads review that differs from yours is added below yours, your text unchanged. Differences in status and rating are shown, and yours are kept.
Your "currently-reading" shelf
Asked: still reading, finished, or did not finish (Goodreads' answer is as old as your export), unless you already have a status on the book here.
Your "to-read" shelf
Your wishlist, with your private notes as the note. Left alone if you already have the book here in any form. A book on your wishlist that Goodreads says you read is asked about.
Your other shelves
Not turned into collections. They are kept with the import, and you can filter the review by them. Rows on an unknown shelf with no reading recorded are skipped unless you say otherwise.
Importing again
Safe: only what is missing is added, and rows you skipped last time stay skipped unless you change them.

Matching only looks at your own books and your household's shelf, never anyone else's. Covers aren't in the export: fetch them after the import. Not imported: additional authors, binding, owned copies, the spoiler flag, and private notes on books that aren't wishlist entries.

Export from Goodreads

  2. Click "Export Library" at the top of the page and wait for the export.
  3. Download the CSV file and upload it below.
