Import from Goodreads

Import your library from a Goodreads CSV export

Please Note

  • Covers aren't in the export. After the import you can fetch them for your new books in one go (it takes a few seconds per book).
  • Details come from Goodreads as they are. Description and genre are empty; edit a book or fetch its details to fill them in.
  • Books you already have are matched by ISBN or title and author, only among your own books and your household's. Nobody else's copy is ever used.
  • Importing again is safe. Books that already have a reading status or wishlist entry are left as they are, so only new rows are added.
  • Not imported: additional authors (mostly translators and illustrators), binding, owned copies and the spoiler flag. Private notes are kept only for wishlist books.

Where Your Goodreads Data Goes

read, currently-reading
Your library, with that reading status. Read Count becomes that many reading sessions; Goodreads only keeps the date of the last one, so earlier reads show as "Date unknown".
did-not-finish, dnf, abandoned
Your library with the Did Not Finish status. Also recognised: gave-up, unfinished, stopped-reading.
to-read
Your wishlist. If you or your household already own the book, it's marked Want to Read instead.
Your other shelves
Each one (for example audiobook) becomes a custom collection of the same name.
Ratings and reviews
Imported on any shelf, including reviews without a rating. Formatting is turned into plain text with paragraph breaks.

How to Export from Goodreads

  1. Go to Goodreads Import/Export
  2. Click "Export Library" at the top of the page
  3. Wait for the export to complete (you'll receive an email)
  4. Download the CSV file from the link in the email
  5. Upload it below
